BelAjar 🎓

Your whole degree journey, one dashboard.*

BelAjar is a student-focused academic management system designed to help university students organise, manage, and track their entire degree journey in one place.

From monitoring academic progress and managing assignments to tracking schedules and staying on top of important academic tasks, BelAjar brings everything together in one centralised dashboard.

 ✨ Key Features

- Dashboard Overview – Get a quick overview of your academic journey.
- Academic Progress Tracking – Monitor your semester progress and academic performance.
- Task Management – Organise assignments, projects, and deadlines.
- Schedule Management – Keep track of classes and important academic activities.
- Academic Analytics – Visualise your progress through charts and statistics.
- Personalised Experience – Customise your dashboard to suit your preferences.

🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Bootstrap 5
- Chart.js

🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the project folder in Visual Studio Code.
3. Open `index.html` in your browser.
4. Start exploring BelAjar!

🎯 Project Purpose

BelAjar aims to simplify student life by providing a centralised platform for academic organisation, productivity, and progress tracking.

Instead of switching between multiple tools, students can manage their degree journey through one dashboard.
